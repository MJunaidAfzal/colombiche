How It Began

Something Special Brewing in Forest Hills

Colombiche was born from a simple wish: to share the coffee and comfort food of Colombia with our Queens neighbors.

We took over the corner of 73-01 Yellowstone Boulevard, filled it with light, marble tables and Colombian art, and opened the doors under a green awning. Today the pastry case holds pan de bono and empanadas, the kitchen serves calentado and daily lunch, and the espresso machine hardly rests.

Our motto says it all: Mountain Vibes · Forest Hills.

Our Philosophy

We believe in real Colombian coffee, food that tastes like home, and a café that feels like part of the neighborhood.
